co-author-experiment.txt
========================

Stumbled onto a GitHub party trick today.

Apparently any commit that ships with a `Co-authored-by:` trailer shows
up on the named human's contribution graph, regardless of whether they
have ever heard of your project. So in theory I can credit literally
anyone — Linus Torvalds, your favourite indie hacker, the goose who
maintains that one Postgres extension — and a small green square
appears on their profile, no consent required.

This commit is the experiment. If you somehow ended up here from
Linus Torvalds's contribution graph wondering when on earth he started
contributing to a Svelte + Tauri spreadsheet editor: he didn't. Hi
Linus, we appreciate you, please don't sue.

(For everyone else: this is a fun curiosity in a personal repo and a
mildly antisocial move in someone else's. Don't be the person who
spam-credits strangers to harvest profile squares.)
